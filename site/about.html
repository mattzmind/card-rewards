About Lucro

Rewards you've earned shouldn't be left at the register

Most people carry two or more rewards cards and still pay with the wrong one. The rules are confusing, they change every quarter, and the difference adds up to real money every year. Lucro fixes that in one tap.

Our mission

Make every card in your wallet work as hard as you do

Lucro started with one question asked at a checkout line: "Which card should I use here?" The answer depends on the store's category, rotating 5% bonuses, categories you picked, spending caps, and what points are actually worth. Lucro keeps track of all of it so you don't have to.

The name comes from the word for profit in Spanish and Portuguese, because that's the point: a little more back on everything you already buy.

What we believe

The principles behind Lucro

Private by design

No account, no bank login, no card numbers. Your wallet lives on your phone, and we never see it.

Independent answers

The best card for you is the one we show. Rankings are never for sale, and no bank pays to be placed higher.

Honest about limits

Reward terms change. We show when each card's terms were last checked and remind you to confirm with your issuer.

Simple on purpose

One question, one clear answer, in the time it takes to reach for your wallet. Built for one thumb at a checkout line.

90+ cards, 25+ banks

Chase, American Express, Capital One, Citi, Discover, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, U.S. Bank and more, with store rules for 200+ merchants.

Kept up to date

When banks change their terms or announce new quarterly categories, Lucro's card data is updated, and the app picks it up automatically.

Real people behind it

Lucro is built by a small independent team. Questions or ideas? Write to us at support@lucro.example. We read every message.
