# Design references (studied Oct 2026)

Direction: **clean corporate, Quicken-led**, in Lucro's brand. The other sites contribute specific patterns only.

| Site | What we took | Where it shows up |
|---|---|---|
| **Quicken** (lead) | White 80px nav, logo left, plain links, one primary pill CTA. Centered hero on a soft tinted band: eyebrow line ("20+ million customers since 1984"), **two-tone headline** (brand color phrase + ink), heavy weight with tight tracking (60px / -2px), one pill button, then a product composite with floating stat cards and an award badge. Sections alternate white / light tint. Split rows: eyebrow → two-tone heading → short body → pill button, product art opposite. | Nav, hero, feature rows, overall rhythm |
| **x.ai** (now light) | Announcement pill above the headline ("New · Meet Grok …"), black pill primary + light-gray pill secondary, rounded product cards in a grid with soft backgrounds. Restraint: lots of white space, few colors. | "Coming soon" pill, buttons, How-it-works cards |
| **Robinhood** | Thin accent-color promo strip across the top; single bright accent used sparingly; "Disclosures" link right under the CTA. | Lime beta strip, disclaimer link in hero |
| **Discord** | Friendly, plain-spoken copy; two big side-by-side CTAs. Privacy page: narrow centered column, very large title, Effective / Last updated dates, numbered table of contents with anchor links. | Copy tone, legal page layout |
| **Quicken Support** | "Need something? We're here to help" header with a search box; content in rounded light cards. | Support page header + FAQ search |
| **Binance.US** | Compact nav with a single high-contrast CTA; separator between logo and links. (Hero didn't render headless.) | Nav CTA treatment |
| **SpaceX** | One statement per full-width band. | The dark privacy band (the only dark section) |

Measured type (desktop H1): Quicken 60/69px w600 -2px · x.ai 60/60px w450 -0.6px · Binance.US 60/65px w700 · Discord 56px w700.
Our H1: Bricolage Grotesque 700, 60px / 1.08, -1.8px tracking (34px on phones).
