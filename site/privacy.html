This policy explains how Lucro ("Lucro", "we", "us") handles information in the Lucro app and on this website. We built Lucro so it works without collecting personal data, and this policy describes exactly how.

1. What we collect

We do not collect personal data through the app. We don't have user accounts, and the app doesn't send your wallet, your searches or your activity to us or to anyone else. This matches the "Data Not Collected" privacy label on our App Store listing.

2. What stays on your device

To work, Lucro saves the following in private storage on your phone. It never leaves your device unless you choose to export it.

  • Which cards you carry, nicknames, and optional last 4 digits
  • Your category picks, quarterly activations, spending caps and favorites
  • Your first name (optional), used only to greet you, and your app settings
  • Recent lookups and simple counts used to show your stats in Profile
  • "Wrong card?" reports you write

Lucro never asks for full card numbers, bank usernames or passwords, and it never connects to your bank accounts.

3. When Lucro uses the internet

The app makes one kind of request on its own: it downloads the public card catalog (card names, reward rules and store categories) so your answers stay current. That request contains no information about you or your wallet. Like any web request, the server that hosts the file can see standard technical details such as your IP address; our host uses them only to deliver the file and protect its service.

If you tap Activate now or View source, Lucro opens your bank's or card issuer's website in your browser. What happens there is covered by that company's privacy policy, not ours.

4. Notifications

Deadline reminders are optional and off until you turn them on. They are scheduled locally on your phone from your wallet. We don't run a notification server and don't receive any device token.

5. Backups and feedback

When you save a backup, Lucro creates a file and hands it to your phone's share sheet. It goes wherever you choose (for example iCloud Drive or Files) and is covered by that service's terms. Backups contain your wallet settings, never card numbers. "Wrong card?" reports stay on your phone unless you choose to share them with us.

6. If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply and to improve Lucro, we don't add you to marketing lists unless you ask (for example, to be told when Lucro launches), and we delete it when it's no longer needed. Please don't send card numbers or passwords.

7. This website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it has no forms that collect data. Our web host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) to operate and secure the site.

8. Children

Lucro is meant for adults who use credit cards. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

9. Your choices

Because your data is stored only on your device, you're always in control. You can edit or remove anything in the app, use Profile, then Erase all data to delete everything, or delete the app. We don't hold a copy, so there's nothing for us to delete or hand over. If you've emailed us and want that correspondence deleted, just ask.

We don't sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under U.S. state privacy laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how Lucro handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change would mean collecting data we don't collect today, we'll explain it clearly in the app before it takes effect.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@lucro.example.
