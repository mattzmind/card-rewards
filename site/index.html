Questions

Good questions, straight answers

Is Lucro really free?

Yes. Picking the best card is free. If we ever add paid extras, the core answer of which card to use will stay free, and rankings will never be for sale.

Why doesn't Lucro ask for my bank login?

It doesn't need it. Knowing which cards you carry is enough to compare their rewards, and keeping your bank login out of the picture keeps you safe.

How does Lucro decide which card wins?

It applies each card's published rewards rules to the store's category, including rotating bonuses, your category picks and spending caps. Points and miles are counted at a conservative cents-per-point value so they compare fairly with cash back.
